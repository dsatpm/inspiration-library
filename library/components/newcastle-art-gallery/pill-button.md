Conversion CTA as a **full-width pill** — max radius, edge to edge across the content column, ~120px tall, heavy grotesk label centred.

The transferable rule: **when a single action matters more than everything around it, scale the button to the content width instead of styling it harder.** No gradient, no shadow, no icon — just size. A conventional button with the same fill would be lost between two loud display headings; at full width it becomes a structural band in the page rhythm.

Note the paired variants elsewhere on the same site: solid black fill for the primary conversion, the identical pill in outline-only for the secondary (newsletter) action. **Same geometry, inverted fill** is a cleaner hierarchy signal than changing size, radius, or colour between the two.

The generous internal padding is what keeps a 120px-tall button from looking like a banner — the label stays optically centred in a large field rather than stretched to fill it.
