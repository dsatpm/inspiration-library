Pull quote as a full section: four lines of display serif at ~3rem in a muted olive on a warm cream ground, attribution far below at ~0.8rem uppercase.

Two rules worth taking:

1. **Set a pull quote in a colour drawn from the brand's environment, not black.** The olive is the vegetation in the site's own photography. Tonal colour at display size reads as considered; pure black reads as a default. Contrast is still comfortably readable because the size is large — large type buys you lower contrast, small type does not.
2. **Split the attribution's two parts by weight, not punctuation.** `RUSSELL MACLAUGHLIN,` in near-black and `NATIONAL GEOGRAPHIC` in grey. The name is the human, the outlet is the credential; different values let the eye take them as two facts instead of one string.

The section is mostly empty space — the quote occupies maybe 40% of the viewport height with nothing else competing. A pull quote that shares a screen with other content is just a paragraph in italics.
