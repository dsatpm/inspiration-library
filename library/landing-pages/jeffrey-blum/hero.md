Three-line headline alternating solid and outlined type: `SHAPE` solid, `VISION.` outlined italic, `BUILD SYSTEMS.` solid. Heavy condensed sans at ~6rem on near-black, with a faint blueprint grid behind.

The transferable rule: **alternate fill and stroke on alternating lines of a display headline to create hierarchy without changing size, weight, or colour.** The outlined line reads as a held breath between two assertions — it visually recedes while staying the same scale, so the rhythm is solid / hollow / solid.

The palette is disciplined: near-black ground, bone-white type, exactly one acid yellow reserved for the single conversion action (`START A PROJECT`) and section eyebrows. One saturated accent used *only* for actions means the user never has to hunt for what is clickable. Stat row (`25+`, `80+`, `Digital + AI`, `∞`) sits directly under the fold line as proof, at a fraction of the headline size.
