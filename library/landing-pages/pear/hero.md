Split hero: transitional serif headline left, a surreal neoclassical painting right — robed figures tending a giant golden pear — with deliberately dithered 1-bit clouds composited into it. Flat ultramarine ground throughout.

The transferable rule: **mixing one high-fidelity historical image style with one low-fidelity digital style produces a stronger identity than either alone.** The dithered clouds and the glitch smear on the pear are the only digital artefacts in an otherwise painterly scene, and that single collision is what makes the page memorable. The technique fails if you use three or more styles — it needs exactly one intruder.

Typographically restrained to let the image carry: serif headline at ~3.5rem, no display sizes, one small black CTA. Thin hairline rules and a small diamond marker in the left margin imply a grid without drawing it. Note the body copy is set *below* the fold line in a separate labelled block (`AT YOUR SERVICE`), keeping the hero to one sentence.
