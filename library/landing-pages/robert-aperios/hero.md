The headline is split across the full viewport diagonal: `CREATIVE` locked to the top-left corner, `DEVELOPER` to the bottom-right, both at ~5rem tight-tracked grotesk. The 3D object floats in the middle. The page frames itself with its own content instead of using a container.

The transferable rule: **anchor a two-word headline to opposite corners and let the empty middle carry the visual.** It reads as one phrase because nothing else on the page uses that size, and the diagonal creates depth a stacked headline cannot.

Second mechanism worth stealing: everything that is *not* the headline is uppercase monospace at ~0.7rem with wide tracking — nav, about paragraph, `LOCATION: ENGLAND`, a live clock. One display face plus one mono face at two extreme sizes is the entire type system. Crosshair `+` marks at the page corners and a warm noise texture over the off-white keep the flat background from reading as unfinished.
