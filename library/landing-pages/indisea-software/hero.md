Hero is pure type on a warm off-white ground: four lines of heavy grotesk at ~6rem, with the single word `maintain` sitting on a solid amber block — a literal highlighter mark.

The transferable rule: **to emphasise one word inside an oversized headline, use a background block rather than a colour change.** Recolouring the word weakens contrast against the ground; a filled block keeps the type at full contrast and adds a second shape to the composition. Reserve it for the word carrying the commercial argument — here `maintain` is the differentiator against firms that only build.

Also note `01 / HERO` in the top-left at ~0.7rem. The whole page is explicitly numbered by section, which turns a long scroll into a legible document and gives every anchor a name. Cheap to implement, disproportionately orienting.
