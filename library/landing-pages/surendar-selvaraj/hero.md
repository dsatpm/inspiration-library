Name set as the headline in a high-contrast display serif at ~5rem, across two lines — `Surendar` in near-white, `Selvaraj` a step darker in grey.

The transferable rule: **when a headline is a single proper noun split over two lines, drop the second line's value by one step instead of keeping both at full contrast.** It gives the block an internal gradient, reads as one unit rather than two shouted words, and lets the eye land on the first line first.

Three CTAs demonstrate correct button hierarchy in a single row: filled white (`View work`) > outlined in accent amber (`Get in touch`) > ghost on ground (`Resume`). Three distinct visual weights for three distinct intent levels — no size change needed. The portrait is rendered as a particle field that dissolves into the dark ground, so it needs no containing frame and the left column keeps its air.
