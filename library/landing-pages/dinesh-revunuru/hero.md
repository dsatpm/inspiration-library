Hero is a rounded off-white card inset from the page edges rather than a full-bleed band, with a cut-out portrait breaking past the card's top edge.

The transferable rule: **let one element break the container's boundary to give a flat card depth.** The portrait overflowing the top of the card does more for dimensionality than any shadow, and costs nothing but `overflow: visible`.

Type pairing: serif for the greeting (`Hi, I'm Dinesh,`), heavy geometric sans in pink for the role (`Sr. Product Designer`). Two faces, two roles — the serif is the human, the sans is the job title. Below the hero, a full-width pill banner introduces a secondary action; keeping it visually distinct from the hero card (different fill, full pill radius) stops it competing for the primary read.
