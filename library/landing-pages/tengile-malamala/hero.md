Full-bleed footage hero where the headline is the only structural element. Display serif at roughly 8rem set in three staggered lines — each line indented further right — turns a flat sentence into a diagonal that follows the river in the footage. The type is doing composition work, not just labelling.

The transferable rule: **when the background image has a dominant diagonal or leading line, break the headline across lines so the ragged edge runs parallel to it.** Centred or flush-left type fights the photo; a staggered rag reinforces it.

Supporting move — the nav is stripped to three anchors (`ENQUIRE` left, wordmark centre, `MENU` right) at ~0.75rem, so nothing competes with the display type. Sub-copy and CTA sit together in the lower right at body scale, giving a ~10:1 display-to-body ratio that makes the hierarchy unmissable without a single weight change.
