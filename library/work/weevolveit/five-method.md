Section opener for the horizontal method track: bracketed label `[ THE FRAMEWORK ]`, a ~5rem monospace headline, a centred paragraph, then the five step names in a single dotted line with the final one in magenta.

The transferable rule: **before a long or unconventional section, show the full set of steps as a single line of text.** `DISCOVER · DIAGNOSE · DESIGN · DELIVER · EVOLVE` tells the reader the scope in one glance, so the horizontal scroll that follows has a known length. Pairs with the progress rail inside the track ([[method-horizontal-scroll]]) — preview before, position during.

This is one of the few places the site centres its type. **Centring is reserved for section openers and used nowhere else**, which makes it a structural signal rather than a style. Centred text is at its worst for long copy and at its best for a short, symmetrical announcement like this.

The whole section inverts to a light ground while the site's hero is near-black. Full theme inversion as a section boundary is the strongest divider available and needs no rule or spacer.

Bracket-wrapped labels (`[ THE FRAMEWORK ]`, `[ TALK TO US ]`) are the consistent eyebrow device across the site — a small repeated typographic convention that costs nothing and reads as authored.
