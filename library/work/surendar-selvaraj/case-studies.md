Case study as a split card: device photograph left, everything else right — `FLAGSHIP PROJECT` eyebrow, serif title, client and year, a four-line summary, a metric, tag chips, and the link.

The mechanism to take: **one metric, set in the accent colour at ~2rem, immediately after the summary.** `70%+ — Of WSJ readers on mobile, the format's starting point`. A single number in a different colour and size is the only thing in the card that competes with the title. Portfolio case studies that list responsibilities without a number are unfalsifiable; one metric makes the whole card credible.

The summary states **role, dates, and what shipped** — *"As Lead UX Architect from February to November 2024, I mapped the user journeys, designed and documented the Vertical Player component… It now ships live in the WSJ Mobile App."* Scope, duration, artefact, current status. Four facts, one paragraph.

Tag chips (`Product Design`, `Multimedia`, `Design Systems`, `Responsive`) at the card's base give filterable metadata without a filter UI.

`01` is set in the image's top-right corner — indexing the case studies against the `All case studies →` link establishes that this is a curated subset.
