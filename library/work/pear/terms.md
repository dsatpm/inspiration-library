The transferable rule: **evolve one illustrated motif through the page instead of introducing a new image per section.** The pear appears in the hero as a monument, here as a construction site. The reader tracks a narrative rather than a slideshow of unrelated art. One motif, several states, is a far stronger system than several motifs.

Ground shifts from the hero's ultramarine to aged, foxed paper with visible stains and grain. **Texture substituting for colour as the section signal** — the layout, type, and spacing are unchanged, only the surface differs.

Copy structure is the takeaway for any value-based pricing page: headline states the model (`No fees. A share of the upside.`), a large paragraph states what the client does not pay, and a small `FULL DISCLOSURE` chip labels a smaller paragraph containing the actual mechanics and constraints. **Label the fine print explicitly and set it smaller but adjacent** — hiding it elsewhere reads as evasive, giving it equal weight buries the offer.
