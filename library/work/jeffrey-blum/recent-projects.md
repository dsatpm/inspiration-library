Project showcase as a single edge-to-edge strip of screens with **no gutters** — dozens of interface captures butted directly against each other, ending in a large photographic mockup that bleeds off the right edge.

The transferable rule: **when the claim is volume of work, remove the gutters.** Individual framed thumbnails invite inspection of each item and expose weak ones; a seamless mass reads as a body of work and is judged as a texture. Use this when the argument is "I have shipped a great deal," never when the argument is "look at this one thing."

The scale ramp across the strip is deliberate — small dense captures on the left growing to one large mockup on the right. It gives the eye an exit point and stops the strip reading as wallpaper.

Section heading repeats the site's solid/outline pair (`RECENT` solid, `PROJECTS` outlined). `VIEW ALL WORK →` sits right-aligned well above the strip, in the empty band between heading and content — placing it there rather than below means it is visible before the reader commits to scanning.
