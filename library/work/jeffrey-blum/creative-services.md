Services as a 3×2 grid of cells separated by hairline rules, with a single acid-yellow rule capping the top of the grid.

Rules worth taking:

1. **One accent-coloured rule above a neutral grid is enough to brand the whole block.** The dividers between cells stay dim grey; only the top edge is yellow. Colouring every divider would flatten the emphasis.
2. Each cell follows a fixed template: zero-padded index (`01`), a thin line icon, a condensed title, one line of description, then small bordered tag chips. **Repeating an identical internal template across all cells is what makes a grid read as a system**; varying the contents per cell is the usual failure.
3. Line icons at a consistent stroke weight and size, all monochrome. Filled or multicolour icons at this size would pull focus from the titles.

The intro paragraph is offset to the right column rather than sitting under the heading — the heading occupies the left, the paragraph the right, and the vertical gap between them is left empty. Same staggered-column device as [[about-editorial]], applied at section-header scale.
