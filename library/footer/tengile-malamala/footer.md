Five-column footer on a solid olive ground, entering directly beneath a full-bleed photograph with no divider.

The transferable rule: **a flat colour change is a stronger footer boundary than a rule or a gap.** The photo ends, the olive begins — the eye reads a new zone immediately. Borders and margins between a media section and a footer are both weaker and more work.

Column structure is grouped by **entity, not by link type**: Explore / Company / MalaMala Game Reserve / Tengile Game Reserve / Connect. Each reserve gets its own address and its own social links. **When a brand covers multiple physical properties, group footer links by property rather than pooling all socials into one row** — it answers "which one am I looking at" without a page change.

Column headers at ~0.75rem uppercase grey; links at ~1rem in near-white. Two-step hierarchy, no bold, no dividers between columns — the whitespace does it.

Bottom row carries copyright left, legal centre, and credits right (`Website by…`, `Brand by…`). Crediting the studios is standard practice in this tier and worth keeping.
