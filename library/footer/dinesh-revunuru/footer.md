Full-bleed saturated blue footer entering hard off white with no transition. Single left-aligned column: name, role, city, one sentence of intent, email, phone, then three text-only social links.

Rules:

1. **Single-column, left-aligned footers are correct for personal sites.** A four-column link grid on a portfolio implies a site map that doesn't exist. Content follows one reading order: who → where → why contact → how.
2. **Contact details as plain selectable text.** No icons, no buttons. Email and phone at ~1.25rem, directly copyable.
3. The framing sentence — *"If you are thinking about hiring me or would like to discuss a project, get in touch with me at,"* — states the intended use of the contact details. One sentence that qualifies inbound messages.

Bottom-right carries `scroll on — the footer hides bubble wrap ↓` at ~0.8rem. **A hint that there is something below the footer is what makes an easter egg discoverable** — an unhinted one is just dead code. Low-cost personality in the one region of a page where nothing else is competing.
