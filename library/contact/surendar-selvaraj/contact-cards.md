No form at all. A centred serif headline, an availability pill, then **four equal channel cards** — Email, LinkedIn, Medium, Figma — each showing the label, the actual handle, and an action link.

The transferable rule: **show the destination, not just the platform name.** Each card exposes the real address (`designbysuren@gmail.com`, `/in/surendarselvaraj`) rather than hiding it behind an icon. The user can copy it, verify it, or click it. Icon-only social rows force a click to discover where they lead.

The outlined status pill — `EXPLORING STAFF AND PRINCIPAL PRODUCT DESIGN ROLES` in ~0.7rem tracked mono, with a small amber dot — is the highest-value element on the page. **A one-line statement of what you currently want, placed above the contact options, filters and directs every inbound message.** The dot reads as a live indicator, implying the statement is current.
