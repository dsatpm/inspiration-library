Rules worth taking:

1. **Crop the wordmark at the page bottom deliberately.** Letterforms running off the edge read as confident scale; the same wordmark fitted neatly inside the margins reads as a logo placement. Only works with heavy weights and short names.
2. **Inline email capture beats a form.** A single underlined field with `SEND` at its right end, sitting in the contact column at body size. One field, no labels above it, no button styling — the lowest-friction capture there is.
3. Three columns with a clear job each: pitch / navigation + language / contact + capture. Thin hairline rules between them and small square corner markers imply a drafting grid without a visible container.
