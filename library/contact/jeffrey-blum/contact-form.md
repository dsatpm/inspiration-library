Two-column contact: pitch and email address left, form right. Form fields are **underline-only** — no boxes, no fills, just a hairline rule under each field with the label above it in ~0.7rem tracked uppercase.

The transferable rule: **on a dark editorial page, boxed inputs import a UI aesthetic that fights the layout; underline inputs stay typographic.** Labels sit permanently above the line rather than as placeholders inside it, so nothing disappears when the user starts typing — the accessibility failure that kills most minimal forms.

Two details worth stealing:
- `Response within 24–48 hours.` sits immediately beside the submit button. **Stating the response time at the point of submission** removes the main reason people abandon a contact form, and costs one line.
- The email address is repeated at display size (`JEFFREY@BLUMJEFFREY.COM`, condensed caps, ~2rem) beneath the pitch. Offering a route that bypasses the form entirely converts the people who distrust forms.

Submit is the page's only acid-yellow block — consistent with the accent-equals-action rule established in [[hero]].
