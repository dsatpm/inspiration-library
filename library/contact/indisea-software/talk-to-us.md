Full-bleed saturated cyan section, near-black grotesk at ~7rem across three lines, contact details inline beneath.

The transferable rule: **flood the contact section with a saturated brand colour while the rest of the page stays neutral.** After a long scroll on warm off-white, the colour change alone signals "this is the end and this is the action" — no divider, no heading, no scroll cue needed. Section identity by colour block is more effective than section identity by label.

Contrast discipline: dark type on saturated cyan, not white. White on mid-saturation cyan would sit near the contrast floor; near-black keeps it comfortably legible at every size including the small underlined email and phone.

Contact details are **plain underlined text at body size** rather than buttons — email, phone, and one small dark pill for the primary action. Three routes, one emphasised. Continues the site's `11 / TALK TO US` numbering so the final section is still part of the document.
