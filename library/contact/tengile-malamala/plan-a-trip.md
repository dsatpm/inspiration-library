Contact section as a full-bleed landscape photograph with the text block placed in the right half — over the water, which is the flattest and darkest region of the image.

The transferable rule: **place text over the quietest region of a photograph instead of adding a scrim.** A gradient overlay would have muddied the image; positioning the block where the subject matter is already low-contrast keeps the photo at full strength. This requires choosing the crop and the text position together — it is an art-direction decision, not a CSS one.

Hierarchy inside the block: `PLAN A TRIP` eyebrow at ~0.8rem, serif statement at ~1.75rem, supporting sans paragraph at ~0.9rem, then a small white button. Four steps down in four elements, all left-aligned to a single edge.

Note the button is small and understated. In luxury hospitality the call to action is deliberately quiet — a loud CTA on this page would contradict the positioning the rest of the design is buying.
