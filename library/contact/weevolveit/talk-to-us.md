Contact channels as two wide bordered cards (email, WhatsApp) with a leading icon, a label, and the actual address — each with a trailing arrow on the right edge.

The rule for the cards: **a full-width channel card with the address visible and the affordance (arrow) pinned right gives a large, obvious target while still showing the destination.** Same principle as [[contact-cards]], different geometry.

The more distinctive move is the type set on a **circular path** across the top of the section, scrolling horizontally as the page moves — `...THEY NEED TO BE RIGHT. YOUR TECHNOLOGY DOESN'T...` in tracked mono with one word in magenta. The transferable rule: **arc-set type works as a section divider when it is a continuous marquee rather than a static curve.** Static arc type reads as a badge or a seal; in motion it reads as a band of atmosphere, and the curve is what stops it looking like a stock ticker.

Only one word in the arc is accented, so the eye gets a fixed point to track as the band moves.
