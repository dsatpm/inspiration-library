Classic 50/50 about split — portrait left, text right — with three details that lift it above the default.

1. **The section opener is a claim, not a label.** `I design products people choose to explore, and complex tools they learn to trust.` at ~3rem serif. The word "About" appears only as a ~0.7rem mono eyebrow in brackets. The transferable rule: **make the largest text in a section the argument, and demote the section name to a tag.** Readers skim headings; give them something worth skimming.
2. The portrait card has generous corner radius and a small amber location badge overlapping its bottom edge — again the break-the-container trick that gives a flat image depth.
3. A vertical tick rail on the right edge labels the current section. Persistent orientation without a sticky nav bar eating vertical space.

Bio is two paragraphs at ~1rem with ~1.7 line-height and a measure of roughly 60 characters. Long-form credibility copy needs the narrow measure; full-width bio text is the most common failure in this section.
