Edge-to-edge black-and-white portrait on the left half, bleeding to the viewport edge with no margin or radius. Text right.

The transferable rule: **a portrait that bleeds off the page edge reads as environment; a portrait in a card reads as an avatar.** Pick deliberately. For a personal brand claiming authority, the bleed is stronger — it makes the subject part of the page rather than an asset placed on it.

Headline repeats the site's fill/outline alternation (`WHERE IDEAS` solid, `BECOME` outlined, `SYSTEMS` solid), which is what makes the identity feel like a system rather than a set of one-off treatments — the same device appeared in [[hero]]. Body copy is deliberately low-contrast grey on black at a narrow measure; the acid-yellow block button is the only saturated element, and it is the only thing to click.

Desaturating the photography to black and white is what lets a single accent colour dominate. A colour portrait here would have competed with the yellow and the page would have two focal points.
