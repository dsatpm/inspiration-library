`03 / WHO WE ARE`. A two-paragraph positioning statement at ~1.75rem — large for body copy, which is the point: this is the page's argument, not supporting text. Credibility proper nouns (`Avalara`, `Google`) get the same amber highlighter treatment as the hero's key verb, so the marker becomes a consistent semantic device across the page rather than a one-off flourish.

The transferable rule: **if you introduce a decorative emphasis device, define what it means and apply it only to that.** Here the marker means "the thing you should remember from this sentence." Used twice on one page, in the hero and here, it teaches the reader its meaning.

Below, four stat cards on a near-white fill. Numbers are **zero-padded** (`012`, `025+`, `006`) so all four occupy similar width and the row reads as a set. Labels sit beneath at ~0.7rem uppercase. Cheap trick, large payoff: without padding, `12` next to `100%` makes the row look misaligned.

The vertical accent bar entering from above ties the section to the one before it — a continuous thread down the page instead of isolated blocks.
